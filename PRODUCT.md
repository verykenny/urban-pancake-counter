# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lorcana card game players, 2–4 people sitting together at a table. They open this app on a phone or tablet mid-game to track lore scores in real time. Context: low ambient light, distracted attention, quick glances between turns. The primary task on any screen is reading and updating a score.

## Product Purpose

A real-time shared scoreboard for Lorcana. No accounts, no card management, no persistence — join a game code, track lore, close the tab when done. Success means a player can glance at the screen and know who's winning, and tap +/− without breaking game flow.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, competitive, tense. The UI should feel like the stakes are real — not gamified or playful, not a generic utility. The Lorcana fantasy world provides the visual register; the competitive tension is the emotional register.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards (white cards, metric grids, clean-serif). Mobile gaming excess (neons, aggressive gradients, casino-style reward UI). Sports broadcast overlays (ticker fonts, TV-score aesthetics). The design should feel like it belongs at the table, not on a leaderboard screen.

## Design Principles

1. **Legibility over atmosphere.** Fantasy theming is welcome; it must never cost readability at a glance.
2. **Scores first.** Every layout decision subordinates secondary chrome to the score numerals.
3. **Low friction, every tap.** Score controls must be large enough to hit without looking twice, especially on mobile.
4. **Earned drama.** Motion and visual effects mark meaningful moments (winning, scoring a big lore gain) — not ambient decoration.
5. **Disappear into the game.** The best UI in this context is one players stop noticing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Player colors must be distinguishable without relying on hue alone (shape or pattern supplement for color-blind players). All animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
